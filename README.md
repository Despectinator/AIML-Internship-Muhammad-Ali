## 👋 About Me

This is Muhammad Ali. I'm a Computer Science student currently in my 7th semester, and my main area of interest is AI and Machine Learning. Most of my projects have been focused on applying these concepts to practical problems. For example, I worked on **InsuGenie**, an AI-powered diabetes monitoring platform involving machine learning and computer vision, and I've also built a **spam email classifier** where I compared multiple ML algorithms. More recently, I developed a **customer intelligence platform** using K-Means clustering for customer segmentation. Alongside ML, I have a strong software development background, particularly in Python, which helps me turn ML concepts into working applications. I'm now looking for an AI/ML role where I can apply what I've learned to real-world problems and continue developing my skills.

This repository tracks my work through a structured **6-week AI/ML Internship Program**, from classic ML fundamentals through deep learning, GenAI, and a final capstone project. 🚀
